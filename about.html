Why we exist

It started with a simple question

Why did the spices on supermarket shelves taste so faint? The answer, we learned, was time and distance: ground months ago, shipped across the world, bulked out with fillers and left to fade.

So we built Saffra to do the opposite — work directly with growers, mill in small batches, and ship spices while they still sing with aroma. Nothing added, nothing hidden.

How we source

From the right farm to your kitchen

Great spice is grown, not manufactured. Here's how every jar makes its journey.

1

We meet the growers

We travel to the regions famous for each spice and build relationships directly with the families who farm them — no faceless middlemen.

2

Single-origin selection

We choose one region per spice for its soil, climate and tradition — Erode turmeric, Tellicherry pepper, Idukki cardamom — so each has a true sense of place.

3

Harvested at peak

Spices are picked when their essential oils are highest, then sun-dried and hand-sorted by the growers themselves.

4

Fair, direct trade

We buy direct and pay above market rates, with long-term commitments that let farming families plan and invest.

5

Sealed for freshness

Whole spices are sealed at source in aroma-lock packaging and milled only when you order.

What we do

Stone-milled, in small batches

We grind on traditional stone mills that turn slowly and stay cool. That gentle, low-heat grind protects the delicate volatile oils that carry a spice's aroma and flavour — the very things high-speed industrial mills burn away.
